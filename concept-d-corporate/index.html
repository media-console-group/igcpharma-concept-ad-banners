CALMA: a Phase 2 Trial in Alzheimer’s Agitation

  • Multicenter, randomized, double-blind and placebo-controlled. IGC-AD1 or placebo, twice daily for six weeks.
  • Primary endpoint: change in agitation from baseline at week 6 (CMAI)
  • 168 randomized, including planned over-enrollment
  • No results reported yet
